Privacy

What this site does with your data

The website itself

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from a third party — the fonts, the styles, the scripts and every photograph are served from this domain. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The web host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs including IP addresses. That is outside my control and is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you send an enquiry

The enquiry form is not connected on this build. Any email you send is held in my inbox and used only to reply to you.

Your details are never sold, never added to a mailing list, and never shared with venues, planners or anyone else.

Your photographs

Photographs from your wedding are never published — not on this site, not on social media, not in a competition entry — without your explicit written permission. That permission is opt-in in the contract, and you can withdraw it later.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, and I will do it. Email is the fastest route.

This notice will be reviewed when the site goes live and should be checked by the business owner against current UK GDPR guidance.
