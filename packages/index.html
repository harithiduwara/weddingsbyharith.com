How booking works

A signed contract and an advance holds your date. The balance is due before the wedding, and I am happy to split it across as many payments as suits you. Ask. It is a normal thing to ask and I would much rather you did than quietly booked somebody cheaper.

Travel outside the Western Province is charged at cost and agreed in writing before you commit. Nothing else is hiding anywhere.

Prices shown are from the 2024/25 guide. If you are reading this a long way into the future, message me and I will confirm where they stand.
