How booking works

A signed contract and 25% holds your date. The rest is due four weeks before, and I am happy to split that across as many payments as suits you. Say so on the call; it is a normal thing to ask and I would rather you asked than quietly booked somebody cheaper.

There are no extras hiding anywhere. Travel outside the Western Province is the only variable and it is agreed in writing before you commit, at cost, with no markup on it.

If none of these three shapes fits your wedding, that is normal and not a problem. Tell me what you are actually doing and I will quote it.
