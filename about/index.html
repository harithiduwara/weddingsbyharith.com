About

I am the one you will forget is there

I have been photographing weddings since 2016. Before that I shot news, which is where the habit of standing still and waiting comes from.

Documentary photography is not a style you apply in editing. It is a set of decisions you make on the day: to use the light that is there rather than the light you brought, to keep working while everyone eats, and to trust that the real thing happening in front of you is more interesting than anything you could arrange.

What that means in practice is that I am early, quiet, and slightly obsessive about preparation. I will have walked your venue. I will know what time the sun clears the treeline. I will have your timeline in my pocket. None of which you should ever have to think about.

Family groups are the one exception, and I do them gladly — quickly, in one place, from a list we agree in advance. Fifteen minutes, not an hour. You will want them in twenty years.

Away from weddings I am usually walking somewhere with a film camera and no particular plan, which is a habit that has never once made me money and has never once felt wasted.
